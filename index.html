MIDSep. 15-18

Enbridge launches open season for West Texas Express natgas pipeline

Proposed ~150+ mile, up to 2 Bcf/d greenfield intrastate pipeline from Waha Hub (Permian) to greater El Paso area (possible laterals to Hudspeth County / Mexico border). Targets power gen, utilities, data centers, industrial demand in West Texas/Southwest/Mexico. Initial capacity up to 2 Bcf/d; target in-service Q4 2029 (subject to support/approvals). Open season: Sept 10–25, 2026 (non-binding). Direct sources: Reuters Sep 15; RBN Energy blog Sep 18.

Reuters / RBN
REGSep. 18

BLM announces November 18, 2026 oil & gas lease sale

30 parcels totaling 8,071 acres across New Mexico (Carlsbad/Farmington), Oklahoma, Texas, Kansas. Scoping completed; public comment closed; 30-day protest period opened Sept 18 (closes Oct 19). Standard process; parcels include resource protections. Leasing is initial step (APDs, etc. follow). Direct source: BLM press release, Sept 18, 2026.
