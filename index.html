M&A / Midstream

Plains All American agrees to acquire Silver Creek Midstream Powder River Basin crude gathering assets for ~$585M

Definitive agreement by wholly owned subsidiary to buy SCM PR II, LLC from Tailwater Capital and The Energy and Minerals Group subsidiaries. Cash consideration ~$585M. Expected close Q4 2026, subject to HSR antitrust clearance and customary conditions. Assets: ~600 miles crude oil gathering/transmission pipelines; >350,000 bpd operating capacity; ~1.2M barrels operational storage; 49% non-operated interest in Powder River Gateway JV (Iron Horse, Powder River Express). Serves diversified Powder River producers; ~125k bpd current throughput; long-term acreage dedications/MVCs (weighted avg contract tenor >8 years); ~915k dedicated acres. Strategic: Expands Plains' Rockies footprint and wellhead connectivity; enhances access to integrated network to Cushing; synergies expected; accretive to long-term earnings/cash flow per CEO Willie Chiang. IRR thresholds met (300-500 bps above cost of capital). Direct source: Globe Newswire / Plains announcement, published 2026-09-16 12:00 UTC. URL: https://boereport.com/2026/09/16/plains-to-acquire-powder-river-basin-assets-from-silver-creek-midstream/ (mirrors company release).

Upstream / Acquisition

BKV completes Barnett shale upstream, midstream and CCS acquisition (closed Sep 15)

BKV Corp. closed previously announced deal for Barnett shale upstream, midstream, and CCS assets in Texas (primarily Montague County). Funded with cash on hand and revolving credit. Terms not disclosed. Adds: >65 MMcfe/d production (>50% liquids, low-decline); ~117,000 net acres; ~0.35 Tcfe PDP reserves; ~1,000 gross operated wells; operated midstream (gas plant 180 MMcf/d capacity, ~340 miles gas gathering, ~225 miles water gathering, 2 saltwater disposal wells); operated CCS project (captured >100k metric tons CO2 trailing 12 months through Q1 2026). Impact: Increases scale in Barnett; expands gas volumes for DFW and Gulf Coast demand; integrates with existing ops/infra; strengthens closed-loop energy strategy (upstream/midstream/CCS). CEO Chris Kalnin: "strong operational fit... low-decline production and reserves." Direct source: World Oil report (Sep 16, 2026). URL: https://www.worldoil.com/news/2026/9/16/bkv-completes-barnett-shale-acquisition-adding-65-mmcfe-d/
