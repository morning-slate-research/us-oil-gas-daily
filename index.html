FERC Infrastructure Actions

FERC actions to expedite natural gas infrastructure

Immediate two-year waiver of Order 871 (allows construction while rehearing pending); temporary increase in blanket certificate cost limits for modifications without case-by-case approval (NOI for permanent); revisions to NEPA procedures for more efficient permitting per Trump EO. Specific approvals include Gulf South/Texas Gas Kosciusko Junction Pipeline (~1.175 MMdth/d incremental capacity) and others. Chairman Christie: "New and expanded natural gas infrastructure is essential to help America avoid a grid reliability crisis." Direct sources: FERC.gov news releases and meeting summaries, September 10, 2026.
