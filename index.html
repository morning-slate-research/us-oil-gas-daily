Transaction detail

Expand’s Twin Eagle agreement broadens its commercial stack ↗

Expand Energy announced an agreement to acquire natural-gas marketer Twin Eagle from Five Point Infrastructure for $1.25 billion. The company said closing is expected in Q3 2026, subject to customary conditions.

  • Expand said it expects the transaction to increase its annual marketing-and-commercial free-cash-flow target by 50% to $750 million.
  • Published reporting said cash on hand and revolver borrowings are expected funding sources.

Why it matters: The pending combination puts more weight on connecting production with transportation, storage, nominations and marketed volumes. No realized synergy, customer-volume, or integration outcome should be assumed before closing.

LNG infrastructure

CP2 order is a Gulf Coast gas-demand execution signal, not added capacity today ↗

Baker Hughes said it secured a major LNG technology order for Venture Global’s CP2 LNG expansion. Trade reporting identified the Louisiana project and described liquefaction/refrigerant technology equipment.

Why it matters: The order is relevant to longer-run Gulf Coast gas demand and upstream-to-market flow assumptions. It does not establish added capacity in service, a contract value, or delivery timing.

Oil & markets

A sharp crude reversal changes cash-flow sensitivity—not verified field output ↗

Reuters reporting syndicated July 27 said oil settled at its lowest level in more than a week after the United States paused attacks on Iran. Contemporaneous reports characterized both WTI and Brent declines as greater than 5%.

Why it matters: The move affects near-term hedge marks, revenue sensitivity and drilling-completion economics. No exchange-primary settlement series was captured for this brief, so no specific benchmark settlement is presented here.
