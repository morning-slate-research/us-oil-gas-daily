Western Gateway Pipeline reaches FID, with a 2029 target subject to approvals.

Phillips 66, Kinder Morgan and HF Sinclair finalized their joint venture and approved the proposed 1,300-mile refined-products system. The companies describe 230,000 bpd of capacity from St. Louis and Gulf Coast supply points to Arizona and California, with completion targeted for 2029, subject to permits and regulatory approvals.

FINAL DECISIONAug. 11

Phillips 66, Kinder Morgan and HF Sinclair finalize Western Gateway JV and FID

The announced ownership is Phillips 66 at 49.9%, Kinder Morgan at 35.1%, and HF Sinclair at 15%. The companies put enterprise value at about $5.0 billion. The proposed system remains subject to permits and regulatory approvals.

GAS OUTLOOKAug. 11

EIA forecasts 3,985 Bcf in October storage and lowers near-term Henry Hub outlook

EIA projects October inventories at 3,985 Bcf, which it says would be the highest pre-winter level since 2016. It forecasts 3Q Henry Hub at $2.87/MMBtu, $0.50 below its July forecast. This is an outlook, not spot-market data.

EIA release
LNG OUTLOOKAug. 11

EIA trims 3Q U.S. LNG export forecast amid Freeport maintenance

EIA forecasts U.S. LNG exports at 16.5 Bcf/d in 3Q, slightly below its July outlook because of Freeport maintenance. Its roughly $85/bbl 3Q Brent forecast is conditional on Strait of Hormuz transit constraints continuing through August.

LEASINGAug. 11

BLM opens comments on 132 proposed northern Arizona oil-and-gas parcels

The 30-day comment period covers 132 parcels totaling 287,284 acres that could be offered in December 2026 and February 2027 lease sales. Comments close September 9. This is neither a lease sale nor a drilling approval.

Briefing notes

Project status

FID does not remove permitting and regulatory conditions

The partners announced a final investment decision and a 2029 target, while expressly making completion subject to permits and regulatory approvals.

Market caveat

Treat all displayed commodity figures as EIA forecasts

The gas, Brent and LNG figures are EIA’s August outlook estimates. The WTI graphic below the controls is deliberately illustrative and is not a historical price series.

Federal process

Arizona parcels are in the public-comment stage only

BLM has invited feedback on potential lease-sale parcels. No lease has been sold and no drilling approval is implied by the notice.
