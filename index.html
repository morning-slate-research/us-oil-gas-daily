Pending transactionDelaware Basin

Matador signs $1.275 billion Paloma Permian acquisition and a separate Woodford acreage transaction

Event date / source publication date: July 23, 2026.

  • Matador Resources entered a definitive agreement to acquire EnCap-backed Paloma Permian LLC for $1.275 billion cash, subject to customary adjustments. Matador expects closing in 4Q 2026.
  • The Paloma package comprises 16,235 net undeveloped acres in Eddy and Lea Counties, New Mexico; Matador estimated 3Q production of about 11,100 BOE/d (57% oil). It cited 55 MMBOE of proved reserves and $816 million PV-10 as of May 31, 2026.
  • Matador also agreed to acquire primarily undeveloped Woodford acreage from EnCap portfolio company Ridge Runner Resources II. Separate consideration was not disclosed. Matador said the transaction, prior additions and its land strategy would bring its Woodford position to about 50,000 contiguous net acres and its Delaware Basin acreage to about 240,000 net acres.

Why it matters: This pending consolidation adds operated inventory, producing wells and development planning in the Delaware. A 4Q close would be a discrete integration milestone for land, well/master data, production allocation, reserves, midstream connectivity and field reporting; it is not yet a closed acquisition.

Public source: Matador announcement via Business Wire

Closed transactionOilfield services

Expro completes Enhanced Drilling acquisition for approximately $215 million

Event date / source publication date: July 23, 2026.

  • Expro completed its previously announced acquisition of Enhanced Well Technologies Group AS (Enhanced Drilling) for approximately NOK 2.0 billion cash (about $215 million), plus customary closing and working-capital adjustments.
  • The acquired portfolio includes managed-pressure drilling, controlled-mud-level drilling and riserless mud-recovery technologies. Expro identified Enhanced Drilling operations in offshore Norway and the Gulf of America.

Why it matters: The transaction expands a major well-services supplier’s managed-pressure-drilling offering in the U.S. Gulf offshore market. Affected operators may have a more consolidated drilling-service interface, increasing the value of consistent well-construction, pressure-management and service-performance records.

Public source: Expro completion announcement
