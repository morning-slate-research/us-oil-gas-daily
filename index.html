Permian Midstream M&A

ONEOK to acquire Brazos Midstream’s Permian Midland Basin assets for $4.425 billion

ONEOK, Inc. (NYSE: OKE) executed definitive agreement to acquire Brazos Midstream’s natural gas gathering and processing assets in the Permian Midland Basin for $4.425 billion cash. Assets include ~700 miles gathering infrastructure, backed by ~600,000 dedicated acres under long-term contracts (weighted avg. remaining term >12 years); processing capacity expanding to ~1.2 Bcf/d by 2027 (incl. Cassidy II). Expected to more than double ONEOK’s Midland Basin processing capacity to ~2.3 Bcf/d. Funding: Separate $9 billion nonvoting minority equity investment from funds/affiliates managed by Apollo (NYSE: APO) into ONEOK Holdings structure (capped 7.0% IRR first 9 years; Class B interest 15% of OpCo quarterly cash flow). ONEOK plans to use ~$5 billion of proceeds to extinguish existing indebtedness (target pro forma 2027 leverage ~3.25x debt/EBITDA; no common equity issuance). Strategic: Immediately accretive to earnings and free cash flow per share; supports mid- to high-single-digit adjusted EBITDA growth target over next 5–7 years. Enhances wellhead-to-water integration with existing NGL/crude infrastructure (e.g., West Texas NGL Pipeline, Medford fractionation). AMI with key producer. Status: Announced Aug 30, 2026. Acquisition expected close Q4 2026 (subject to customary conditions incl. Hart-Scott-Rodino clearance; unanimously approved by ONEOK Board). Apollo minority equity investment expected close first half of September 2026. Direct Sources: ONEOK PR Newswire, Apollo release, ONEOK IR/press Aug 30, 2026; Rigzone coverage Sep 1, 2026.

Haynesville Upstream / Midstream

Comstock Resources enters $1.65B LOI with SOCAR for Haynesville upstream + midstream stakes + separate $450M drilling JV with Jerry Jones family

LOI (not yet definitive PSA): SOCAR (or sub) acquires for $1.65B cash (effective Jul 1, 2026): 20% non-op WI in Legacy Haynesville upstream; 15% non-op WI in Western Haynesville (steps down to 7.5% after 5 years once 15% ROI achieved); 15% of Comstock's 73% ownership in Pinnacle Gas Services LLC (midstream for Western Haynesville). Target definitive by Oct 31, 2026; close by YE 2026 (subject to negotiations, gov't/third-party approvals, customary conditions). Benefits: Validates asset value; introduces investment-grade international partner (SOCAR) for balance sheet strength + global LNG marketing; net debt pro forma from $3.1B to ~$1.5B (Jun 30, 2026 basis); accelerates Western Haynesville development (545,000 net acres); retains operatorship/control. SOCAR gets pro-rata future participation rights + Comstock gas marketing help. Separate drilling JV (announced same day): Jones family partnership funds 85% of 18 Western Haynesville wells + 80% of 9 Legacy wells over next 12 months starting Sep 1, 2026 (~$450M total cost). After 15% ROI, 50% interest reverts to Comstock. Supports delineation without straining balance sheet; feeds midstream volumes. Direct sources: Comstock GlobeNewswire press release (Sep 1, 2026, 07:30 ET); CRK 8-K filing (Sep 1, 2026).

Permian Midstream M&A

Enbridge enters definitive agreement to acquire Salt Creek Midstream's crude oil gathering business for $600 million

Assets: 100% of Orla and Wink North + 50% interest in Delaware Crossing (DCX) system in core Delaware Basin (Permian). ~500 miles of crude gathering infrastructure serving >20 producers supported by ~320,000 net dedicated acres under long-term agreements (avg. remaining life ~10 years). Combined throughput capacity 420,000 bpd; 350,000 bbl storage. Delivers into multiple long-haul Permian pipelines incl. Enbridge's majority-owned Gray Oak. Strategic: Extends Enbridge's Permian presence; enables wellhead-to-water integration via Gray Oak, Cactus II, and Enbridge Ingleside Energy Center (North America's largest crude export terminal). Financial: Expected immediately accretive to distributable cash flow per share and EPS. 2026 financial guidance unchanged. Timeline/conditions: Close later in 2026, subject to customary conditions including FTC clearance under Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act. Direct sources: Enbridge Inc. press release (PR Newswire/CNW), Aug. 26, 2026 and enbridge.com. Reuters confirmation, Aug. 26, 2026. (Coverage into early Sep 2026 per packet.)
