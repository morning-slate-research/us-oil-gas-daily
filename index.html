DOE official · Sep. 29

DOE issues RFP for final 40 MMbbl SPR exchange.

U.S. Department of Energy issued Request for Proposal for up to 40 million barrels of crude from Strategic Petroleum Reserve via exchange (Big Hill and Bryan Mound sites). This completes the U.S. 172 MMbbl tranche of the IEA-coordinated ~400 MMbbl global release tied to Iran-related supply disruptions and elevated fuel prices. Deliveries Nov/Dec 2026; companies return barrels plus premium (prior exchanges showed ~25% premium). SPR inventory at multi-decade lows (~283.8 MMbbl as of recent week). Bids due 11:00 AM CT Oct 6, 2026.

Comstock Resources & SOCAR advance $1.65B Haynesville partnership framework

Comstock and Azerbaijan’s SOCAR signed framework advancing strategic partnership. SOCAR to invest $1.65B for non-operated working interests in Comstock’s Haynesville assets (20% Legacy, 15% Western reducing to 7.5% after 5yrs at 15% ROI) plus 15% of midstream Pinnacle Gas Services. LNG / INTLSep. 29

LNG Canada Phase 2 FID; doubles capacity from 14 to 28 MMtpa

Shell and JV partners (Shell 40%, PETRONAS 25%, PetroChina 15%, Mitsubishi 15%, KOGAS 5%) took final investment decision on Phase 2 expansion at Kitimat, BC. Doubles capacity; ~C$33B investment. Adds two trains, storage, berth, utilities; Coastal GasLink expansions (5 compressor stations). Commercial ops early 2030s. Shell to receive ~6 MMtpa additional. Major private investment; positions Canada as larger LNG exporter. Sources: Shell official, LNG Canada, Canadian PM, Bloomberg, Reuters, World Oil (Sep 29).
