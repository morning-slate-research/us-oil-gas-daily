Record Labor Day Fuel Prices
AAA / CNBC · Sep. 7FX / FXEmpire · Sep. 7

Record Labor Day gasoline prices hit $4.15/gallon amid supply constraints.

National average regular gasoline reached $4.15/gallon on Sept 7 (record for Labor Day; prior record $3.82 in 2012 per AAA). Diesel hit ~$5.90/gallon (also record). Driven by elevated crude (WTI ~$89-92/bbl, Brent ~$96-97/bbl) and disruptions from U.S.-Iran tensions, including weekend tanker strikes in/near Strait of Hormuz and sharply reduced tanker traffic (avg ~10 ships/day, lowest since May). OPEC+ left October output policy unchanged (~Sept 6 meeting). Winter-blend transition in Sept may provide some relief. Direct sources: AAA data via CNBC (Sept 7), CBS News/AP (Sept 7), FXStreet/FXEmpire (Sept 7 oil market).

REGULATORYSep. 8

BLM proposes streamlined permitting rule for Alaska NPR-A production sites

Bureau of Land Management proposed rule (public inspection ~Sept 4-7; Federal Register publication Sept 8) to create criteria-based, streamlined process for qualifying production sites (pads, wells, roads, pipelines) and associated ROWs in the ~23-million-acre National Petroleum Reserve-Alaska. Targets sites within ~25 miles of existing permanent infrastructure; aims for 60-day decision after complete application using broader EIS. 60-day public comment period to Nov 9, 2026. Direct sources: Federal Register (2026-18261, Sept 8 pub.), Alaska Beacon (Sept 5/7).

INVESTMENTSep. 7

South Korea advancing $22.3B Texas gas-fired power investment

Reports Sept 7-8 indicate South Korea selected/advancing a 6.3 GW combined-cycle gas-fired power plant project in Encinal, Texas (phased; initial ~1.4 GW gas turbine + additional combined-cycle), as the first major project under its ~$350 billion U.S. investment commitment. Aimed at surging power demand from AI data centers and semiconductors in Texas. Cost rose from initial ~$19.8B estimates. Formal announcement expected soon. Sources: Reuters (Sept 7), BusinessKorea (Sept 7), Seoul Economic Daily/Korea Herald (Sept 7-8).

MIDSTREAM M&ASep. 3

Williams closes $5.5B Momentum Midstream acquisition (Haynesville)

Williams (WMB) closed acquisition of Momentum Midstream (~$3.5B cash/debt + ~$2B equity). Adds >4,000 miles of pipelines, >1M dedicated acres, 6 Bcf/d gathering capacity, processing/treating facilities, and 4.05 Bcf/d take-or-pay transportation capacity in the Haynesville. Strengthens integrated platform connecting supply to Gulf Coast LNG, power, and industrial demand. Expands presence in fastest-growing U.S. natural gas supply basin. Direct source: Williams Companies press release (Sept 3, 2026).
