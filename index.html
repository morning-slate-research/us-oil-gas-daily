DEVON / WHITEWATERPBOG · Aug. 21

WhiteWater-led JV reaches positive FID on Solitude Pipeline System for Permian-to-Katy gas takeaway.

WhiteWater-led JV (WhiteWater 50% equity, Devon Energy 25%, MPLX 10%, Diamondback Energy 7.5%, Western Midstream Partners 7.5%) reached positive FID on two 48-inch natural gas pipelines connecting Permian Basin to Katy, TX. Phased buildout: initial ~2.25 Bcfd capacity entering service in second half of 2029; additional 2.25 Bcfd in 2030 (target total 4.5 Bcfd by 2030, with further expansion potential). Supported by significant long-term firm transportation agreements. Addresses Waha hub volatility for associated gas; supports Gulf Coast demand including LNG exports and power. Devon has secured related LNG-linked offtake (100 MMcf/d starting 2027, +150 MMcf/d in 2028). Subject to customary regulatory approvals.
