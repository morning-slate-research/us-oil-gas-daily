Texas RRC approves ExxonMobil Rose CCS project (2-1 vote)

Texas Railroad Commission approves permit for three Class VI injection wells in Jefferson County, Southeast TX (Fleming and Upper Frio formations). Capacity: up to 5 million metric tons CO2 per year (or ~53 million tons over 13-year period) from industrial sources via planned 18-mile pipeline. Includes monitoring wells; MRV plan previously approved by EPA. Project on 13,000 acres private land; storage 0.5-1.5 miles deep. Dissent by Commissioner Wayne Christian citing long-term safety concerns, lack of convincing purpose for underground injection, and taxpayer subsidies (45Q tax credit). Clears major regulatory hurdle for ExxonMobil Gulf Coast CCS ambitions. Direct sources: Texas RRC official statement (Sept 15, 2026); JPT SPE (Sept 17, 2026).

Regulatory / CCS

Texas Railroad Commission approves ExxonMobil Rose CCS project

Texas RRC approves permit for three Class VI injection wells in Jefferson County, Southeast TX (Fleming and Upper Frio formations) by 2-1 vote on September 15, 2026. Capacity: up to 5 million metric tons CO2 per year (or ~53 million tons over 13-year period) from industrial sources via planned 18-mile pipeline. Includes monitoring wells; MRV plan previously approved by EPA (April 2025 per reports). Project on 13,000 acres private land; storage 0.5-1.5 miles deep. Dissent by Commissioner Wayne Christian citing long-term safety concerns, lack of convincing purpose for underground injection, and taxpayer subsidies (45Q tax credit). Clears major regulatory hurdle for ExxonMobil Gulf Coast CCS ambitions. Direct sources: Texas RRC official statement (Sept 15, 2026) — https://www.rrc.state.tx.us/news/commissioner-christian-votes-no-on-carbon-capture-permit/; JPT SPE (Sept 17, 2026) — https://jpt.spe.org/texas-regulator-oks-exxonmobil-ccs-project; also Bloomberg, Enerdata, USA Herald reports (Sept 15-17).
