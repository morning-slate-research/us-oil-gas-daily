FERC proposes historic blanket authorization program for LNG facilities and streamlined gas project reviews.

Unanimous NOPR establishes 3-tier blanket auth for LNG plants/terminals under NGA Sections 3/7: automatic for qualifying activities (no capacity increase, within footprint, design limits); 60-day prior notice for activities that may increase capacity but stay within footprint/limits; 90-day prior notice for new facilities like liquefaction trains, LNG tanks, marine berths. Separate draft policy statement (4-1 vote): eliminate routine requirement for air dispersion modeling in NEPA reviews of natural gas projects; shift to streamlined approach avoiding duplication with EPA/state Clean Air Act processes. Aimed at reducing bottlenecks for gas infrastructure to support power generation and LNG exports. Proposal only — not final rule. Comments due 60 days after Federal Register publication. Direct sources: FERC.gov (Oct 15), Utility Dive (Oct 16).
