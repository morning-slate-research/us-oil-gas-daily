Magnolia agrees to acquire WildFire Energy for approximately $4.06 billion

Event date / source publication date: July 19, 2026 / July 20, 2026.

  • Buyer: Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation and Magnolia Oil & Gas Operating LLC. Seller: WildFire Energy I LLC. The signed purchase agreement covers 100% of WildFire Intermediate Holdings, LLC.
  • The transaction is valued at approximately $4.06 billion, inclusive of WildFire debt and subject to customary adjustments. Consideration includes $2.65 billion cash, 32.203 million Magnolia Class A shares, and the assumption of $600 million of 7.50% senior notes due 2029.
  • The assets add approximately 810,000 net acres in Giddings, raising Magnolia’s pro forma Giddings position above 1.25 million net acres across the Eagle Ford, Austin Chalk and Woodbine. WildFire contributes approximately 53,000 Boe/d, about 70% oil, plus a sand mine and more than 500 miles of Giddings gas-gathering pipelines.
  • Status: announced as a definitive agreement; Magnolia expects closing late in Q3 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. Its estimate of at least $100 million in annual synergies/cost savings is forward-looking, not a realized result.

Why it matters: This is a material South Texas consolidation of adjacent acreage, wells and field infrastructure. If it closes, the 53 Mboe/d asset integration will require disciplined reconciliation of well and lease masters, ownership and revenue data, production allocation, gathering records, field workflows and accounting controls.

Public sources: Magnolia release furnished with Form 8-K · Magnolia Form 8-K
