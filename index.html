M&A / MIDSTREAM

bp divests $1.5B non-controlling interests in U.S. onshore midstream to Sixth Street (Oct 6, 2026)

bpx energy (bp’s US onshore) sells non-controlling stakes in Permian and Eagle Ford midstream assets (pipelines and facilities, including four Permian central processing facilities: Grand Slam, Bingo, Checkmate, Crossroads). bpx remains operator of all assets. Transaction: two phases — ~$1 billion upon signing; balance by end of year, subject to regulatory approvals. Post-close ownership: Permian midstream to 51% (from 100%); Eagle Ford to 25% (from 75%). Sixth Street holds remaining non-operating interests. Part of bp’s divestment program targeting $20 billion by end-2027. Direct source: bp press release (via bp.com); Oilfield Technology coverage dated 2026-10-06. Confirmed facts only; transaction not yet closed.

MIDSTREAM

Summit Midstream advances Double E Pipeline compression expansion in Permian Basin (reported Oct 6, 2026; FID Aug 31, 2026)

900 MMcf/d compression expansion (bidirectional mainline compressor station) on Double E Pipeline (135 miles from Eddy County, NM/Delaware to Waha hub). Open season secured ~550 MMcf/d binding long-term commitments to Waha; total contracted firm capacity ~2.2 Bcf/d. Advance discussions for remaining capacity. In-service: 4Q 2028. Cost: ~$100 million net to Summit’s 70% interest. Addresses intrabasin gas movement to Waha amid Permian infrastructure buildout. Direct source: East Daley Analytics report (2026-10-06).
