Briefing notes

Transaction detail

Matador signs Paloma Permian acquisition and a separate Woodford acreage transaction

Matador Resources entered a definitive agreement to acquire EnCap-backed Paloma Permian LLC for $1.275 billion cash, subject to customary adjustments. Matador expects closing in 4Q 2026.

  • Paloma comprises 16,235 net undeveloped acres in Eddy and Lea Counties, New Mexico, with estimated 3Q production of about 11,100 BOE/d, 57% oil.
  • Matador also agreed to acquire primarily undeveloped Woodford acreage from Ridge Runner Resources II. Separate consideration was not disclosed.

Why it matters: A closing would create a discrete integration milestone for land, well-master data, production allocation, reserves, midstream connectivity, and field reporting. This remains a pending transaction.
