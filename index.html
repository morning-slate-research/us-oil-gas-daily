EIAOfficial weekly release · Aug. 13

Lower-48 gas storage rises 36 Bcf, inventories stay 6.7% above the five-year average.

EIA reported 3,153 Bcf of working gas for the week ended Aug. 7, up 36 Bcf from the prior week. Inventories were 198 Bcf above the five-year average and 25 Bcf below the year-ago level.

Weekly imports increased and exports fell. The EIA estimate describes that reporting week, not a confirmed lasting trend. The WTI controls and graphic remain illustrative, not historical data.
