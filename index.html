American Press / Center Square · Sep. 28

FERC issues Final EIS for Cheniere Sabine Pass Stage 5 LNG expansion.

FERC staff issued Final Environmental Impact Statement for proposed expansion at Sabine Pass LNG terminal (Cameron Parish, LA). Project includes three new liquefaction trains (Trains 7-9, ~300 Bcf/y each or ~19.5 MTPA total addition) and 55.6-mile Sabine Crossing Pipeline. Current terminal ~30 MTPA; expansion would increase export capacity significantly. FERC: "limited environmental effects" mostly temporary/short-term during construction; to be mitigated to less-than-significant levels. Construction targeted late 2026; initial operations ~2030. Ship traffic projected to rise from 580 to 740 carriers/year. Related: Louisiana Board approved "mega-project" status for ~$6B expansion; pending tax exemption approval.

FTAI Infrastructure / Jefferson Energy to acquire USD Group crude logistics assets for ~$255M

FTAI Energy Partners LLC (Jefferson) subsidiary entered definitive agreement to acquire Port Arthur Terminal (Port Arthur, TX; ~50,000 bpd rail crude handling + 12-mile pipeline to P66 Beaumont terminal) and 50% interest in Diluent Recovery Unit (Hardisty, Alberta) from USD Group subsidiary. Total consideration ~$255M cash. Expected to generate ~$50M annual EBITDA next 12 months. Assets support long-term take-or-pay contract with major E&P company (investment-grade counterparty). Closing subject to regulatory approvals, expected Q4 2026. Source: GlobeNewswire (Sep 28).

Comstock Resources & SOCAR advance $1.65B Haynesville partnership framework

Framework agreement advancing previously announced strategic partnership. SOCAR to invest $1.65B in Comstock's Haynesville shale assets (key U.S. natgas basin for LNG feed). Commitment to negotiate definitive purchase/sale agreement (target signature by Oct 31, 2026; close by YE 2026). Also exploring opportunities for SOCAR to market attributable natgas as LNG internationally outside U.S. Part of broader U.S.-Azerbaijan energy ties. Sources: World Oil, GlobeNewswire (Sep 28).
