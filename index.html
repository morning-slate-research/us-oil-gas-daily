FERC proposes first blanket authorization program for LNG infrastructure.

Unanimous vote Oct 15 (reported Oct 19). Three-tiered framework: automatic authorization (no capacity increase, within footprint/design limits); 60-day prior notice (capacity increase within footprint); 90-day prior notice (new liquefaction trains, tanks, berths, auxiliaries outside footprint, with limits on size/location). Separate draft policy statement on NEPA air quality reviews: no longer requires air dispersion modeling for NGA projects (streamlines but continues disclosure of effects). Comments periods open. Direct sources: FERC.gov (Oct 19), Utility Dive (Oct 19).
