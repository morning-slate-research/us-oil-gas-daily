Baker Hughes completes acquisition of Chart Industries

Event date / source publication date: July 16, 2026.

  • Baker Hughes completed its acquisition of Chart; Chart became an indirect Baker Hughes subsidiary.
  • Chart shareholders received $210.00 cash per share.
  • Baker Hughes stated that Chart will operate as a third reporting segment focused on air and gas handling, thermal management, and lifecycle services.
  • The company targets $325 million in annualized cost synergies by the third year after close; that is a forward-looking target, not a realized result.

Why it matters: This is not an upstream-asset transfer or an immediate production-data conversion. It consolidates a significant supplier footprint across gas-infrastructure, thermal-management and lifecycle services, which can affect future vendor, equipment and maintenance workflows for US gas and LNG-adjacent projects.

Public sources: Baker Hughes Form 8-K — merger completion · Baker Hughes completion release (Exhibit 99.1)

BLM reports more than $4.1 billion from second-quarter federal oil-and-gas lease sales

Event date / source publication date: July 16, 2026.

  • For April–June 2026, BLM reported more than $4.1 billion in receipts from 389 parcels totaling 355,456 acres.
  • BLM said most receipts came from New Mexico. Its May New Mexico/Texas sale generated more than $4 billion and sold 74 parcels covering 33,530 acres.
  • BLM reported $4.7 billion in 2026 lease-sale receipts through the second quarter, including $592.7 million in Q1.

Why it matters: Lease awards do not guarantee drilling, but the scale signals intense competition for federal acreage—especially in New Mexico/Texas—and creates future leasehold, obligation, permitting, ownership and regulatory-reporting records to administer.

Public source: Bureau of Land Management release
