Permian Midstream M&A
ENBRIDGE Reuters · Aug. 26

Enbridge enters definitive agreement to acquire Salt Creek Midstream's crude oil gathering business for $600 million.

Assets: 100% of Orla and Wink North + 50% interest in Delaware Crossing (DCX) system in core Delaware Basin (Permian). ~500 miles of crude gathering infrastructure serving >20 producers supported by ~320,000 net dedicated acres (avg. remaining life ~10 years). Combined throughput capacity 420,000 bpd; 350,000 bbl storage. Delivers into multiple long-haul Permian pipelines incl. Enbridge's majority-owned Gray Oak. Strategic: extends Enbridge's Permian presence; enables wellhead-to-water integration via Gray Oak, Cactus II, and Enbridge Ingleside Energy Center. Expected immediately accretive to distributable cash flow per share and EPS. 2026 financial guidance unchanged. Close later in 2026, subject to FTC clearance under Hart-Scott-Rodino Act.
