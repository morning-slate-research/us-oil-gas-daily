Devon records 1.359 MMboe/d in its first full combined-company quarter

Devon reported 2Q production of 1.359 MMboe/d, including 503 MBbl/d of oil, and $1.269B of capital expenditures. The company reported 120 net operated wells brought online, 34 operated rigs and 10 completion crews during the quarter.

Devon / SEC
ASSET ADDITIONAug 4

New Mexico lease-sale acreage adds Delaware locations to Devon inventory

Devon said it acquired 16,300 net acres in the core Delaware Basin for $2.6B through the New Mexico federal lease sale. It said the block adds about 400 top-tier locations with 87.5% NRI and that development is planned to start in 2027.

Briefing notes

Production-data workflow

Separate the Delaware acreage block in land and well records

The newly disclosed federal-lease acreage should remain distinct in land, well, drilling, production and economics datasets until development begins. The 2027 start is Devon’s stated plan, not realized activity.

Combined-company signal

Third-quarter volume guidance provides a new planning checkpoint

Devon guided 3Q production to 1.660–1.690 MMboe/d, including 550–560 MBbl/d of oil, with $1.4B–$1.5B of capital spending. Those are management forecasts and should not be recorded as output.

The research packet cites Reuters attributing the selloff to President Trump cancelling a planned attack on Iran. The attribution is reported market context; the daily WTI and Brent values are the verified series facts.
