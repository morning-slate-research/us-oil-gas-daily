Dallas Fed Q3 2026: District oil & gas activity slows but production rises.

Oil and gas activity in the Eleventh District (Texas, northern Louisiana, southern New Mexico) continued to expand, though at a slower pace. Business activity index: 38.8 (down from 46.1 in Q2). Oil production index rose to 20.7 (from 15.0); natural gas production index to 14.8 (from 3.7). Cost pressures elevated (input costs, finding/development, lease operating expenses above averages). Supplier delivery times lengthened (index 36.2). E&P firms more positive on outlook (50.0) than services (4.6); overall uncertainty up slightly. Price expectations (survey avg): WTI $88.38/bbl YE 2026 (range $70–$126); Henry Hub natural gas $3.29/MMBtu YE 2026. Survey period: Sept 16–24; 125 firms responded (83 E&P, 42 services).
