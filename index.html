Antero reports July Marcellus acquisitions and record quarterly production

Antero said it completed about $315 million of strategic acquisitions in its core Marcellus footprint. It reported quarterly net production above 4.1 Bcfe/d, up 21% year over year; sellers and property-level allocations were not identified.

Briefing notes

Production-data workflow

Treat acquired Marcellus output as an attribution change

The disclosed production, acreage and undeveloped locations should be tracked as acquired assets rather than assumed organic growth. Antero raised full-year guidance to 4.15–4.20 Bcfe/d and expects 5 Bcfe of Q3 curtailments.

Infrastructure read-through

Construction is a connectivity signal, not capacity in service

East Side Express belongs in an in-development asset register. Without disclosed scope, permits, capacity or timing, it should not be counted as available takeaway capacity.

Market caveat

Weekly observations are not a July 29 futures settlement

The cited price and refinery figures are EIA’s dated weekly measures. They frame operating and price conditions but do not establish a specific company production change or a single market cause.
