WILLIAMS Investor Release · Sep. 3

Williams closes $5.5B Momentum Midstream acquisition.

Williams (WMB) closed acquisition of Momentum Midstream (~$3.5B cash/debt + ~$2B equity). Adds >4,000 miles of pipelines, >1M dedicated acres, 6 Bcf/d gathering capacity, processing/treating facilities, and 4.05 Bcf/d take-or-pay transportation capacity in the Haynesville. Strengthens integrated platform connecting supply to Gulf Coast LNG, power, and industrial demand. Expands presence in fastest-growing U.S. natural gas supply basin. Direct Sources: Williams investor press release (Sept 3, 2026).

MIDSTREAM M&AAug. 30

ONEOK to acquire Brazos Midstream Permian gas assets for $4.425B

ONEOK agreed to acquire Brazos Midstream's natural gas gathering and processing assets (~700 miles gathering infrastructure, 1.2 Bcf/d processing capacity, ~600k dedicated acres under long-term fixed-fee contracts, supported by 14 active rigs from producers incl. ExxonMobil, Diamondback). More than doubles ONEOK's Midland Basin processing capacity to ~2.3 Bcf/d (incl. future plants). Implied ~7.5x 2027 EBITDA (w/ synergies). Funded by $9B non-voting minority equity investment from Apollo funds/affiliates (expected close H1 Sept 2026 for equity piece); proceeds also to extinguish ~$5B existing debt (no common equity issuance). Expected immediately accretive to EPS and FCF. Close of asset acquisition Q4 2026 subject to HSR and customary conditions. Direct Sources: ONEOK press release and EX-99.1 SEC exhibit (Aug 30, 2026).

UPSTREAM / LOISep. 1

Comstock Resources $1.65B LOI with SOCAR for Haynesville + $450M Jones family JV

Comstock (CRK) entered LOI with SOCAR (Azerbaijan state oil co.) for $1.65B cash for non-operated interests: 20% in Legacy Haynesville upstream assets, 15% in Western Haynesville (reverts to 7.5% after 5 years/15% ROI), and 15% of 73% interest in Pinnacle Gas Services midstream. Effective date July 1, 2026. Targets definitive PSA by Oct 31, closing year-end (subject to approvals). Proceeds to reduce net debt from ~$3.1B to ~$1.5B (pro forma). Comstock retains operatorship; SOCAR gets future participation rights and LNG marketing opportunities for Comstock gas. Separate Haynesville drilling JV: Jones family partnership funds 85% of 18 Western + 80% of 9 Legacy wells (~$450M total cost) over next 12 months starting Sept 1. Direct Sources: Comstock announcement / investor materials (Sept 1, 2026); Reuters (Sept 1, 2026).

FERC issues final EIS for Commonwealth LNG Project

FERC staff issues Final Environmental Impact Statement for proposed 8.4 MTPA liquefaction terminal + associated 3-mile pipeline in Cameron Parish, LA (1.44 Bcf/d supply). Includes storage, marine facilities. FERC staff conclude some adverse impacts (e.g., visual, EJ) but most reducible to less than significant with mitigation; significant on visual and disproportionate EJ impacts noted. Direct Source: FERC announcement (Sept 4, 2026) https://ferc.gov/news-events/news/ferc-staff-issues-final-environmental-impact-statement-commonwealth-lng-project .
