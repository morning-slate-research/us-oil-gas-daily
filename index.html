WhiteWater-led JV reaches FID on Solitude Pipeline System for Permian-to-Katy gas takeaway.

WhiteWater (50%), Devon Energy (25%), MPLX (10%), Diamondback Energy (7.5%), Western Midstream Partners (7.5%) reached positive FID on two 48-inch natural gas pipelines from Permian Basin to Katy, TX. Phased design: initial ~2.25 bcfd service targeted H2 2029, second similar phase 2030. Devon holds firm capacity and is securing LNG-linked pricing (100 MMcfd starting 2027 + 150 MMcfd 2028). Subject to customary regulatory approvals.
