MIDSTREAM M&AAug. 12

Mora Energy closes Tejon, Carbon Solutions and Williams’ Quail system purchases

Mora says the combined Midland Basin platform includes about 200 miles of gas gathering pipe, four compressor stations, an amine treating facility and an acid-gas injection well. It also announced increased NGP equity commitments and a new revolving facility; terms were not disclosed.

Mora release
LNG POLICYAug. 12

Alaska LNG tax and oversight compromise is proposed, not enacted

Gov. Dunleavy sent a compromise bill to the Legislature that includes a 2% flat S-corp tax, effective Jan. 1, 2030 or upon first commercial gas, and removes an education-aid provision. The Legislature is scheduled to reconvene Aug. 20.

Alaska governor
GAS DEMANDAug. 12

Commercial terms support up to 10 GW of gas-fired capacity in Texas and Pennsylvania

NextEra, Commerce and Japan said their definitive agreements support development of up to 10 GW, with Commerce citing an initial $3.3 billion investment. Projects remain subject to permitting, construction and commissioning; initial resources are expected as early as late 2028.
