Briefing notes

Production-data workflow

Separate acquired from organic growth

Chevron’s U.S. production aggregate combines Hess volumes with operating growth. Production systems should preserve portfolio and basin attribution rather than interpreting the full year-over-year gain as organic performance.

Supply reconciliation

Keep revisions and timing in the record

May’s lower production estimate and higher export flow are not a stated causal relationship. Reconcile field output with terminal and marine movements by reporting period, and retain EIA’s revision qualification.

Leading indicator

Use basin rig movement as a watchpoint

The one-rig national gain offers little on its own. Basin-level activity can inform crew and service assumptions, but it should not be converted directly into a production forecast.
