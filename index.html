M&ASep. 16 / 21

Chord Energy divests non-operated Marcellus assets to POSCO for $550M

Sale of ~32,000 net acres (core Marcellus, Pennsylvania); TTM production ~121 MMcfpd (100% residue gas, no NGLs); ~1,000+ wells. Highly accretive: ~6x Adjusted EBITDA (at $3.50/MMBtu Henry Hub assumption). $55 million deposit received; effective date July 1, 2026; expected close Q4 2026. Impacts: Reduces annual capex by ~$25 million; increases oil weighting 4-5 percentage points; further lowers net leverage (already below peers). Portfolio now exclusively focused on Williston Basin. Chord Energy PR Newswire, September 16, 2026. Highlighted in Wood Mackenzie "US upstream in brief" (Sep 21, 2026).
