Gunvor/Silver Hill declined or no immediate comment.

LNG Regulatory

D.C. Circuit upholds FERC approval of Venture Global CP2 LNG terminal and associated pipeline

Decision issued Tuesday, August 25, 2026. Court rejects consolidated challenges by environmental groups (e.g., For a Better Bayou, Sierra Club) and commercial fishermen on NGA public interest determination, NEPA "hard look" (air quality, fishing, climate), and other grounds. Notes high bar under Natural Gas Act for denying projects; recent SCOTUS limits on NEPA scope cited. Venture Global: "Construction continues to advance well and safely... first LNG expected in the second half of 2027." Separate pending lawsuit on DOE authorization for Cameron Parish impacts. Direct source: E&E News by POLITICO (Niina H. Farah), August 25, 2026.

LNG Operations

Cheniere confirms planned maintenance at Corpus Christi LNG export terminal

Company confirmation follows preliminary LSEG data reported by Reuters (Aug 24). Corpus Christi intake ~1.8 bcfd (down from typical ~2.6 bcfd). No duration or scope disclosed. Broader U.S. LNG terminals saw combined feedgas below full processing capacity on Aug 24. Cheniere (largest U.S. LNG exporter) operates Sabine Pass (LA) and Corpus Christi (expansion ongoing). Direct sources: energynews.pro, August 25, 2026; original Reuters, August 24, 2026.

Permian Midstream

Targa Resources / ExxonMobil 20-year Permian midstream agreements (coverage Aug 25)

Fee-based integrated services (gathering, processing, treating, NGL transport/fractionation) across Delaware and Midland sub-basins through 2046, with new acreage dedications. Targa to build three new Delaware gas plants (~825 MMcf/d total, online H1 2028) and Bull Run II ~70-mile pipeline to Waha (take-or-pay). Raises 2026 growth capex estimate to ~$5 billion. Extends/expands prior relationship. Official: Targa Resources Corp. press release, August 17, 2026.
