LNG DEVELOPMENTAug. 10

Survey work is awarded for Delfin’s proposed Louisiana floating LNG port

World Oil reported that TDI-Brooks was awarded geophysical and geotechnical survey work for the Delfin Deepwater Port offshore Louisiana, with acquisition scheduled for late August and geotechnical work for late September. This is development progress, not FID, financing close, or a new offtake commitment.

SERVICESAug. 10

Energy Services of America reports higher transmission revenue, lower backlog

The contractor reported fiscal Q3 revenue of $130.0 million, up from $103.6 million year over year, and cited stronger Gas & Petroleum Transmission activity. Backlog was $286.6 million, down from $325.1 million at March 31; the company also raised its quarterly dividend to $0.04 per share.
