Gulf producers restart shut-in production post-Hurricane Isaias; shut-ins eased to ~57.5% oil (~1.17M bpd) as of Sunday.

Oil and gas producers (BP, Shell, Chevron and others) restarting shut-in production after Hurricane Isaias (landfall Florida panhandle ~Oct 10/11). Shut-ins eased significantly over weekend: ~57.5% of Gulf oil output (~1.17M bpd) still suspended as of Sunday per Marine Minerals Administration (MMA)/BSEE data, down from higher peaks (~63-72%). Personnel returning to platforms; no major damage reported by majors. Direct sources: Seeking Alpha (Oct 12, 2026); Mansfield Energy Natural Gas News (Oct 12); Rigzone cross-ref; BSEE/MMA updates referenced across.
