Briefing notes

M&A / MIDSTREAM

Chevron restructures Bakken midstream; divests Hess Midstream stake and DJ Basin assets (Oct 6, 2026)

Chevron enters definitive agreements with Hess Midstream LP (HESM) to transfer ownership interests in Hess Midstream (incl. GP position) and DJ Basin crude oil midstream assets. In exchange: $200M cash + extended/revised Bakken commercial agreements through 2045 (tariffs reduced ~50%). Chevron expects $3-4B after-tax loss at close (YE 2026); deconsolidates ~$3.7B debt off balance sheet. Hess Midstream to acquire DJ Basin assets (~400k bpd oil gathering, 300 MMcf/d gas gathering, 420k bbl storage + 20% Saddlehorn pipeline interest) and operate as independent multi-basin midstream. Chevron to reduce Bakken rigs from 3 to 2 in Dec 2026. Sources: Chevron press release (Business Wire/SEC 8-K exhibit, Oct 6); Hess Midstream press release (Business Wire, Oct 6); Reuters (Oct 6/7).

M&A / MIDSTREAM

ONEOK completes Brazos Midstream Permian acquisition for ~$4.425B (Oct 7, 2026)

ONEOK, Inc. completes purchase of Brazos Midstream's Permian Midland Basin natural gas gathering and processing assets for ~$4.425B cash. Assets include ~700 miles gathering pipelines, 1.1 Bcf/d processing (+ under construction), 600k dedicated acres under long-term fixed-fee contracts (weighted avg. remaining term >12 years). Doubles ONEOK's Midland Basin processing capacity to ~2.3 Bcf/d. Enhances connection of growing natgas/NGL production to ONEOK's integrated system. Funded in part by prior $9B Apollo minority equity (used to retire ~$5B debt). Source: ONEOK newsroom press release (Oct 6/7, 2026); Gas Processing & LNG (Oct 7).

INVENTORY / DATA

EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report: crude inventories draw 3.2M bbl (Oct 7 release)

U.S. commercial crude oil inventories (ex-SPR) fell 3.2 million barrels to 424.1 million barrels (week ended Oct 2, 2026), vs. analyst expectations of +1.7M build. Gulf Coast draw ~3.9M bbl drove it; Cushing stocks +444,000 bbl. Refinery crude inputs +223k b/d to 16.5M b/d; utilization 92.7% (up 0.2 pts). Crude exports +1.2M b/d to 4.77M b/d; imports +1.1M b/d. Gasoline stocks +382k bbl; distillates unchanged. Stocks ~1% above 5-yr average. Strong exports and refining activity cited. Sources: Reuters (Oct 7, 2026); EIA.
