Line 5 trespass finding stands; Seventh Circuit sends remedies back to district court

The court affirmed the availability of restitution and a permanent injunction for trespass on allotted Bad River Reservation parcels, but vacated the specific restitution award and injunctive relief. It required a reasonable opportunity for rerouting and rejected the prior three-year deadline as too aggressive.

Briefing notes

Production-data workflow

Keep peak capacity distinct from daily production

Record the new wells, water-injection equipment and ownership split in facility and allocation master data. Do not translate bp’s peak annualized measure into an actual field production rate without subsequent production reporting.

Route-continuity risk

Treat the Line 5 timetable as unresolved

The appellate decision leaves reroute execution, remaining permits and the new district-court remedy as the operative watchpoints. It does not establish a new enforceable shutdown date.

Commercial capacity

Add LEAP Phase 5 as contracted, not in-service, capacity

The long-term contracts are a concrete Haynesville takeaway signal. Capacity, contract, nomination and measurement records should retain its 200 MMcf/d expansion status and avoid treating it as operating throughput.
