CONTINENTALPR Newswire · Aug. 20

Continental Resources acquires FireBird Energy II for major Permian Midland Basin expansion.

Continental Resources entered agreement to acquire FireBird Energy II LLC (Quantum Capital portfolio) for significant Permian (Midland Basin) expansion. ~54,000 net acres in Midland Basin; ~147,000 net resource acres across >6 stacked-pay reservoirs; 95% operated. Current production ~32,000 boepd (69% oil); 307 gross operated development locations. Assets adjacent to Continental's existing Permian ops; total Permian acreage grown >40% over past 14 months. Expected close September 2026, subject to customary conditions.

Midstream FID

Solitude Pipeline System FID subject to customary regulatory approvals

WhiteWater-led joint venture (WhiteWater 50%, Devon Energy 25%, MPLX 10%, Diamondback Energy 7.5%, Western Midstream Partners 7.5%) approves construction of two parallel 48-inch natural gas pipelines from Permian Basin to Katy, TX. Phased capacity: Initial 2.25 Bcf/d entering service H2 2029; second phase 2.25 Bcf/d in 2030; total designed up to 4.5 Bcf/d with expansion potential. Backed by long-term firm transportation agreements (predominantly investment-grade shippers). Devon holds firm capacity; aims to move Delaware gas out of volatile Waha hub to Gulf Coast markets tied to LNG export and power generation.
