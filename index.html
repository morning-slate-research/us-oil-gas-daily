WILLIAMS Investor Release · Sep. 3

Williams closes $5.5B Momentum Midstream acquisition.

Williams (WMB) completed acquisition of Momentum Midstream, adding >4,000 miles of pipeline, >1M dedicated acres, 6 Bcf/d gathering capacity, processing/treating facilities, and 4.05 Bcf/d take-or-pay transportation in Haynesville. Transaction ~$3.5B cash/debt + $2B equity. Strengthens position connecting supply to Gulf Coast LNG, power, and industrial demand. Announced Aug 3; closed one month later. Direct Sources: Williams investor press release (Sept 3, 2026).

UPSTREAM M&ASep. 2

Diversified Energy announces $1.8B Birch Permian acquisition

Diversified (DEC) to acquire Birch Permian Holdings (Elliott-backed) for ~$1.8B, adding ~68,000 boe/d net production (38% oil, 32% NGL, 30% gas), ~480 net wells, ~46k net acres, proved reserves ~1.17 Tcfe, integrated midstream/water infrastructure (processing, gathering, disposal). Expected to boost overall production ~35%, Adj. EBITDA ~55%. Largest deal in company's 25-year history. Funded primarily via ~$1.5B Carlyle ABS (PDP-backed) + revolver/other; no large equity raise at announcement. Expands Carlyle partnership to up to $10B future PDP deals. Close expected Q4 2026, subject to approvals. Direct sources: Diversified IR press release (Sept 2, 2026); 8-K filing (Sept 3); Rigzone (Sept 3, 2026).

MIDSTREAM M&AAug. 30

ONEOK to acquire Brazos Midstream Permian gas assets for $4.425B

ONEOK to buy Brazos Midstream's Permian Midland Basin natural gas gathering/processing assets (~700 miles gathering, 1.2 Bcf/d processing, ~600k dedicated acres, long-term fixed-fee contracts, 14 active rigs). More than doubles ONEOK's Midland processing capacity to ~2.3 Bcf/d (incl. future plants). Funded by $9B non-voting minority equity investment from Apollo funds (expected close H1 Sept 2026); use proceeds for acquisition + ~$5B debt extinguishment (no common equity issuance). Expected to be immediately accretive. Brazos acquisition close Q4 2026, subject to HSR/customary conditions. Direct sources: ONEOK IR press release (Aug 30, 2026); 8-K; presentation PDF. Coverage in RBN Energy (Sept 3 blog).

UPSTREAM / LOISep. 1

Comstock Resources $1.65B LOI with SOCAR for Haynesville + $450M Jones family JV

Comstock LOI for SOCAR to acquire non-operated interests (20% Legacy Haynesville upstream, 15% Western Haynesville stepping to 7.5% after 5 yrs/15% ROI, 15% of Pinnacle Gas Services midstream) for $1.65B cash. Effective July 1, 2026. Targets definitive PSA by Oct 31, close year-end. Aims to cut net debt from ~$3.1B to $1.5B. Comstock retains operatorship. Separate $450M drilling JV with Jerry Jones family partnership (funds majority of 27 wells over 12 months starting Sept 1; reversion after 15% ROI). Direct sources: Comstock GlobeNewswire/IR (Sept 1, 2026); Reuters (Sept 1); OGJ (Sept 1).
