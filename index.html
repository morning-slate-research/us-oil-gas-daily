ExxonMobil, the largest U.S. oil producer by volume, operates more than 30 drilling rigs in the Permian Basin, including two automated rigs with robotic equipment. The company aims to automate half its Permian fleet by 2028 to improve safety (reduce dangerous rig-floor work) and efficiency (e.g., one automated rig drilled a two-mile horizontal well in about six days).

Permian Drilling Automation

ExxonMobil advances automated drilling in Permian

ExxonMobil (largest U.S. oil producer by volume) operates more than 30 drilling rigs in the Permian Basin, including two automated rigs with robotic equipment. The company aims to automate half its Permian fleet by 2028 to improve safety by reducing dangerous rig-floor work and to improve efficiency. One example: an automated rig drilled a two-mile horizontal well in about six days. Direct source: Reuters, published August 24, 2026.

Permian Midstream Expansion

Targa Resources announces major Permian gas processing and pipeline expansion

Plans for three new natural gas processing plants (Wrangler, Ranger, and Ranger II) adding 825 MMcf/d combined capacity in the Delaware Basin, plus a new 70-mile Bull Run residue pipeline to the Waha hub. Both facilities and pipeline targeted for first-half 2028 operations. Backed by new 20-year fee-based agreements with ExxonMobil units for gathering, processing, and downstream services; extends prior arrangements to 2046 with new area of mutual interest. Targa also evaluating up to five additional plants contingent on production trends; raised 2026 net growth capital estimate to ~$5 billion. Drivers include rising Permian production, LNG exports, and power demand.

Northeast Pipeline Approval

Federal appeals court upholds New York approval for Williams' $1B+ Northeast Supply Enhancement pipeline

U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit upheld the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation's Clean Water Act Section 401 water-quality certification for the project. The ~37-mile expansion (plus facilities) adds ~400,000 dekatherms per day capacity on Transco's system to serve National Grid customers in New York City and Long Island. Construction groundbreaking April 2026; targeted in-service Q4 2027. Other challenges (New Jersey, federal) remain pending.
