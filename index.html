Kimbell signs $215.4 million affiliated-seller royalty “Drop Down”

Event date / source publication date: July 16, 2026 / July 17, 2026.

  • Kimbell Royalty Partners agreed to acquire certain oil-and-gas mineral, overriding-royalty, royalty and related interests from Rivercrest Capital Partners LP, Rivercrest Capital Partners II LP and Cupola Royalty Direct LLC; the agreement also covers partnership interests in OGM Partners I and RCPTX that Kimbell does not already own.
  • Consideration is approximately $215.4 million: about $74.9 million cash plus 9.5 million Kimbell Royalty Operating, LLC common units valued at about $140.5 million, subject to customary adjustments.
  • The portfolio comprises about 2,568 net royalty acres in the Eagle Ford, Permian, Mid-Continent and Appalachia. Kimbell cited expected Q3 average production of 2,347 boe/d and 177 DUCs and permits.
  • Status: the release says closing remains subject to customary conditions and is expected on or around August 21, 2026; it is not a completed transaction.

Why it matters: A closing would change mineral and royalty ownership across several active basins. Operators and revenue administrators should anticipate interest-deck, division-order, revenue-allocation and asset-master changes rather than treating this as an immediate operating-control transfer.

Public sources: Kimbell Royalty Partners release · Kimbell Form 8-K

Pickton reaches FID on 35-Bcf Northeast Texas gas-storage field and Paris Hub

Event date / source publication date: July 17, 2026.

  • Pickton Gas Storage reached final investment decision on a 35-Bcf natural-gas storage facility and transportation hub in Northeast Texas; commercial operations are expected to begin in spring 2028.
  • Tailwater Capital invested in Pickton and the project secured a construction-financing package. The initial phase is fully contracted; financial terms were not disclosed.
  • The project will convert a depleted reservoir into a high-deliverability field with 35 Bcf of working-gas capacity and add the Paris Hub in Paris, Texas.
  • The hub is planned to have bidirectional interconnections with five large-diameter intrastate and interstate pipelines, linking supply basins with demand centers across Texas and the Gulf Coast.

Why it matters: The project adds future balancing and deliverability optionality in Texas. Producers, marketers and midstream teams will ultimately need new receipt/delivery-point, interconnect, nomination and contract-reference records; FID itself does not create current operating capacity.

Public source: Pipeline & Gas Journal — Pickton FID
